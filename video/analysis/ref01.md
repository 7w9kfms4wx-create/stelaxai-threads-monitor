# ref01 — "Ads Engine" (ElevenLabs Creative) product promo: one ad multiplied into languages, markets and channels, shown as one continuous object that keeps changing form

- 1276×718 (≈16:9), 30 fps, 57.7 s (≈1732 frames), AAC stereo 44.1 kHz.
- Only **2 hard cuts** detected (10.73 s, 23.73 s, both soft). Everything else is one continuous camera-less 2D "morph chain".
- Audio: **male VO over a ~94 BPM music bed**. The spectrogram shows speech formants throughout; the RMS sits around −15 dBFS. The track fades out evenly from 52.3 s (−19 dB) to 57.5 s (−51 dB) under the end logo, about a 5 s tail. See "Sound & sync" below.

## Animation architecture
- **Layers**: (1) bg: either full-bleed photography with a soft blur or a flat warm off-white #F1EFEA; colour washes are heavily blurred (≥100px) versions of the content palette. (2) Objects: one "hero object" at a time (card, deck, tile, orb, row), always centred or balanced on a diagonal pair. (3) Micro-labels: 8–10px grey captions + hairline rule under cards, flag pills (white pill, flag emoji + 9px label). (4) Connectors: 1px #BDBDBD lines with rounded elbows, or long diagonal hairlines that run off-frame.
- **Camera**: pure 2D. "Camera" moves are group translates (pans of 30–50% of frame width, ease-in-out ~25 frames) and continuous slow scale-ups of the hero group (1.0→1.7 over 3 s). No 3D tilt. Depth is faked with blur and scale (DOF on spheres) and with soft shadows.
- **Transition vocabulary**: (a) morph (text→dot→card, orbs→card, card→sphere, row-cell→banner), (b) light-leak/colour-fog diagonal wipes, (c) stack/merge, (d) split/duplicate with connector, (e) hard cut only at the climax of an acceleration. Morph is the default; a cut is the exception.
- **Typography**: one neutral grotesk (Inter/"Eleven" style), regular weight for words, bold only in the logo. Size small and confident (headline ~2.5% of frame height, ~18px at 720p), centred, generous whitespace. Choreography: type-on with per-glyph fade trail; colour tint→neutral settle; logo built by sliding bars; suffix slot-roll.
- **UI simulation**: the dashboard is reduced to one row; counters tick; highlight chips fade in; flag pills as metadata. No cursor.
- **Colour system**: neutral warm-grey base (#F1EFEA), black text, one accent colour borrowed from the content each moment (orange, lavender, sage, teal). Iridescent multi-hue gradients for "AI/abstract" elements (orbs, app tiles).
- **Grain/lighting**: subtle; the cards carry a large soft drop shadow (y≈12px, blur≈30px, ~15% black). The photo bg at the start has a natural grade.

## Pacing & rhythm
- ~16 distinct "beats" in 57 s, so ~3.5 s per idea, but sub-motions happen every 0.5–1 s (feed steps, content swaps, colour fog), so the eye is never idle.
- Structure: Hook (title on colour, 2 s) → Inputs (cards, 4 s) → Multiply (deck accelerating, 4.5 s → cut) → Distribute (tree, 3 s) → Scale (feed, 6 s) → Audio layer (orbs, 3 s) → Variants (split pairs, 10 s) → Funnel → Proof (dashboard metrics, 3 s) → Montage finale (colour cycling, 8 s) → Bookend title → Logo (8 s incl. family roll).
- Tension builds through **acceleration** (deck cadence 10→6 frames) and release through a hard cut to a single calm object. The payoff is the colour-cycling montage + metrics. It ends on the brand with a long, quiet tail and no explicit CTA text.

**Global character**: nothing overshoots. Every move is either an expo in-out for travel (pans, scrolls, logo bars) or a cubic/expo-out for arrivals. Motion blur is not rendered; speed is carried by the 60–80 px/frame peak velocities alone.

## Sound & sync
- **Music**: a mellow, warm electronic/lo-fi pulse at **93.75 BPM** (beat = 19.2 frames, bar = 76.8 frames). It is constant under the VO with no drop. There is a pre-cut "suck-out": the level dips to −25…−29 dB at 10.45–10.70 s, then a hit at −14 dB lands **exactly on the 10.73 s hard cut**. A swell/whoosh rises to −9 dB at 33.35–33.6 s while the cards dissolve into spheres. The music fades out over the last 5 s (52.3→57.5 s).
- **VO** (rough ASR, gist reconstructed): 0.2 s "Introducing [Ads Engine]… your best-performing ad runs in one language, even though your brand sells in dozens of markets — that's worldwide revenue left on the table… [ads] in text and in video, localized [into many languages] while preserving the [brand voice]. But what if…" → 22.4 s "…a brand-new [ad]… localized for [each market]… then over time performance data flows back into the system, [which] generates fresh variants in just a minute and keeps the cycle running…" → 45.7 s "…take your content truly global with Ads Engine, today, now available in ElevenLabs Creative… [ElevenLabs] Productions." The VO narrates the problem → the mechanism → the CTA, and the visuals illustrate each clause literally (one language → flag card; dozens of markets → deck; data flows back → node funnel → dashboard).
- **Cut-on-beat ratio**: automatic motion-onset detection gives 6/30 onsets within ±2 frames of a beat (20%, chance ≈ 21%), and **motion peaks 33% on-beat** (chance 21%). So it is **not frame-locked overall**. The **big moments are locked**: deck landings, the 10.73 s hard cut, pan peak velocity at 14.0 s, feed step at 16.57 s, logo collision at 49.5 s.
- **VO-pause rule**: speech-band gaps at 10.80–11.40 (just after the hard cut), 13.40–13.65 (pan), 32.80–33.35 (cards → spheres), 34.40–34.70 (network converges) and 45.3 s. **Every major morph sits in a VO breath**, while small motions (feed steps, content swaps) run under speech.
- **Sync rules to copy**: (1) place the peak velocity of an in-out move on the beat (not its start). (2) Before a hard cut, duck the music 6–10 dB for ~8 frames and land a hit on the cut frame. (3) Big morphs go in VO pauses of ≥0.25 s. (4) Accelerate events from 1-beat to 1/3-beat spacing to build into a cut.

## Reusable recipes
(At 94 BPM: beat = 19 frames, ½ beat = 10, ⅓ beat = 6.)
0. **Measured house curves**: travel = `Easing.bezier(0.8,0,0.2,1)`; arrival = `Easing.bezier(0.2,0.8,0.2,1)` (≈ spring({damping:56, stiffness:530, mass:1}), no overshoot); exit = `Easing.bezier(0.9,0.2,1,1)`.
1. **Type-on with trailing fade**: reveal 1 char every 2 frames; each char opacity 0→1 over 4 frames (linear), y +2px→0. Colour starts at the accent (#E07A3F) and interpolates to the final colour (#111) over 10 frames after the full word is done.
2. **Light-leak diagonal wipe**: a full-frame linear-gradient overlay at 115°: transparent → #F1EFEA (0.3–0.7 stops) → solid. Translate x from −120% to 0 over 35 frames, `Easing.bezier(0.45,0,0.25,1)`. Under it, crossfade bg A→B.
3. **Speck-to-card pop (measured)**: phase A (morph, ~6 frames): a blurred dot grows to 0.69× with blur 14px→2px. Phase B: scale 0.69→1.0 over **18 frames** with `Easing.bezier(0.21,0.8,0.77,1)` (progress 30% at f2, 49% at f4, 74% at f8) and **no overshoot**. The parent row re-centres with the same curve over ~15 frames (`translateX = -(n-1)*(cardW+gap)/2`). Trigger the start ~2 frames before a beat so the 50% point lands on it.
4. **Accelerating deck push-in (measured)**: landing intervals **[15,15,16,12,6,6,7,5] frames**, i.e. on-beat ×3 then ⅓-beat (at 94 BPM; scale them to your tempo). Each landing: scale 1.06→1, opacity 0→1, motion decaying over 8 frames (`bezier(0.2,0.8,0.2,1)`). Back cards: offset (+10px,−10px), scale 0.94, opacity 0.6. The whole deck scale goes 1→1.7 over the sequence with ease-in (`bezier(0.5,0,0.9,0.6)`). Hard-cut to a calm frame exactly on the next downbeat, with the music ducked 8 frames before the cut.
5. **Branch connectors + dot carriers**: SVG paths with orthogonal elbows (corner r 12px, 1px #BDBDBD). Animate `strokeDashoffset` length→0 over 10 frames ease-out, stagger 8 frames. A 6px accent dot travels the path (same timing). At the end the dot scales into a 160px rounded tile (r 24px) via spring(damping 14, stiffness 180).
6. **Stepped snap feed (measured)**: a vertical list; every **27 frames** translateY −= (cardH+gap) ≈ 58% of frame height, using `Easing.bezier(0.6,0.04,0.29,1)` over **21 frames**, then hold ~6 frames. Peak speed ≈ 85 px/frame at 720p (no motion blur needed). A side marker (flag dot) updates on each step. Scale the period to 1.5 beats of your track.
7. **Gooey merge (metaball)**: 3 circles in a group with an SVG filter `feGaussianBlur stdDeviation=10` + `feColorMatrix` alpha contrast (`0 0 0 18 -7`). Move the circles to the centre over 10 frames (ease-in), then crossfade into a rounded rect (border-radius animating 50%→6%) with the content image fading in on the last 6 frames.
8. **Split-pair variant**: the card scales 1→0.75 and duplicates. Copy A moves to (−22%, −18%) at opacity 0.55 and saturate(0.6). Copy B moves to (+22%, +18%), sharp, shadow `0 12px 30px rgba(0,0,0,.15)`. A 1px diagonal hairline through both runs off-frame. Duration 18 frames, spring(damping 20, stiffness 120).
9. **Colour-fog montage**: the bg is a 200%-size blurred (blur 120px) radial-gradient blob in the next accent colour, translated diagonally across the frame over 15 frames. Swap the hero content at the midpoint (frame 7) with a 6-frame crossfade. Repeat every 30 frames with a new hue.
10. **Logo bar-squeeze + suffix slot-roll (measured, identical in ref03)**: two black bars (w = 0.12·fontSize, h = cap-height) start at ±(wordWidth/2+24px) and close over **15 frames** with `Easing.bezier(0.8,0,0.16,1)` (5% at f4, 75% at f8). The word between them is clipped. **Hold 3 frames** (the collision lands on the beat). The bars open over **20 frames** with `Easing.bezier(0.68,0,0.19,1)` and the letters are revealed by the bar edges (clip-path inset from the centre). Suffix swap inside a line-height overflow mask: the old word exits y 0→−0.7em over **10 frames** with `bezier(0.9,0.2,1,1)` (pure ease-in). The new word enters from +0.55em over **12 frames** with `bezier(0.1,0.08,0.2,1)` ≈ spring({damping:56, stiffness:530}). Hold each suffix ~45 frames (≈2.3 beats).
