## Format
- 1280×720 (16:9), 30 fps, 48.8 s (≈1464 frames), stereo AAC 48 kHz.
- Audio: music-led mix with a quiet, under-mixed VO (see Sound & sync). RMS −12…−17 dB steady; a big low-end hit at **6.3 s** (lowpass energy jumps from −30 to −13 dB for ~2 s) exactly on the only hard cut (to giant type); smaller low hits ≈10.2 s, 11.4 s; fade out from 43 s (−18 → −37 dB by 48 s).
- Hard cuts detected only at 6.3, 24.9, 35.6, 37.4 s. Rest is continuous camera moves over one infinite canvas.

## Animation architecture
- **Layers**: (1) bg: animated grainy cloud texture (video/noise shader) for bookends, flat #EDEDED/#F2F2F2 for the "work" section, pastel radial gradient canvas for zoom-out; (2) canvas plane holding node cards, lines, images (all transformed by one camera); (3) floating overlays: glass agent card, chips, labels; (4) grain overlay on the cloud sections.
- **Camera system**: 2D infinite canvas with one camera transform (translate + scale). Constant gentle motion (never fully static), punctuated by push-ins (×1.5–2.5, ~20f ease-in-out) to read text, and pull-outs to show scale. One hard cut to "inside the text field" macro view on the bass drop.
- **Transition vocabulary**: caret↔card morph (intro and outro), push-in/pull-out, generation-bloom (blur/iridescent → sharp), converge-to-orb collapse, orb-expand-to-bg, blur cross-fade of labels, hard cuts only on musical hits.
- **Typography**: neutral grotesk; UI text tiny (8–12 px at 1280), headline/question text ≈26 px, giant typing ≈90 px. Black on light grey, greys for secondary, orange for "active" line, blue for @mentions. Choreography: typewriter + caret, colour-trailing newest letters, line-by-line log with highlight-then-settle.
- **UI simulation techniques**: typing with caret; picker wheel; skeleton cards → generation bloom; chips with selection flash; list rows staggering; playback counter + waveform progress; pill labels swapping; bezier connectors drawing.
- **Depth/lighting/grain**: film grain on cloud bg (strong, ~8–10% monochrome noise), soft drop shadows on cards (0 8 24 rgba(0,0,0,.08)), frosted glass, iridescent blobs.
- **Colour**: warm orange/peach/violet brand atmosphere for bookends; neutral light-grey working surface; small accents: green status dot, blue/purple/pink node-type colours, orange highlight.

## Pacing & rhythm
- ~49 s; perceived "beats" every 2–4 s (each a new UI state), though hard cuts are only 4. Average beat ≈3 s.
- Structure: Hook (0–6 s: typed name + caret morph + prompt) → DROP (6.3 s giant type) → Build (agent log, pickers, generation, 9–24 s) → Interaction (choices/voice, 25–34 s) → Payoff (full-screen video + collage, 34–39 s) → Collapse & bookend (39–44 s) → Logo (44–49 s).
- Tension builds by increasing density (more nodes, zoom-out) until it collapses into one orb — a satisfying release.
- CTA: "What should we create next?" in the same card, then logo. No URL.

## Sound & sync
- **Music**: bright, airy electronic track with plucks and vocal-like textures, **104 BPM** (1 beat = **17.3 frames**, 1 bar = 69 f). 0–6 s is filtered/low energy; **6.3 s = the drop** (lowpass energy jumps −30 → −13 dB for ~2 s), mid-section steady −12…−17 dB, short dips at 21 s, 23 s, 43.5 s (breaths before section changes), fade-out from 46 s to silence at 48.6 s.
- **Voice**: a quiet VO sits under the music (spectrogram shows speech formants; ASR is rough). Recoverable gist with timestamps: 0–8 s intro line about creating assets; ~8.5 s "…**Flows Agent** … picks the right models…" (exactly when the model-picker wheels play, 12–15 s); ~14 s "…generations"; 17–39 s description of finished product/assets; ~40 s and ~44 s closing line. The VO is under-mixed; music carries the edit.
- **Cut-on-beat ratio**: 5 hard cuts; within ±3 f of a beat: 3/5 (6.30 s +2.7 f, 24.93 s +2.2 f, 37.67 s −1.0 f); within ±2 f: 1/5. The two collage cuts at 35.6/37.43 are off-grid (+7 f / −8 f) — they ride the **onsets** (35.74, 37.09 plucks) instead. Key animation starts on the grid: canvas collapse 40.00 s (on beat 40.00 and strong hit 39.97), card→logo 44.0 s (−1.8 f from beat+hit 44.06).
- **SFX**: no separate whoosh library; the cut to giant type sits on the drop (a bass/sub hit), the collapse sits on a strong hit, and the logo split sits on the last strong hit 44.06.
- **Sync rules**: (1) the single hard cut of the hook lands on the drop, ≤3 f late (cut slightly *after* the downbeat feels intentional); (2) section transitions start on the beat, then continue through the bar; (3) typing speed is independent of the beat (≈13 chars/s) — texture, not rhythm; (4) VO names the feature while the matching UI is on screen ("right models" ↔ picker wheels).

## Reusable recipes
1. **Caret → card morph** (intro/outro): caret 2×22px. Measured: overdamped spring `spring({damping:20, stiffness:40, mass:1})` (slow 6–10 f start, 96% at ~45 f, tail to 65 f) — or `cubic-bezier(0.6,0,0.1,1)` over 60 f: width 2→440, height 22→75, borderRadius 1→14, bg white; letters within `|x - caretX| < cardWidth/2` get opacity→0 with a 2f chromatic tint (pink/green text-shadow ±1px). Inner field & buttons scale 0.6→1 with the card; header fades in at f+4 over 6f. Reverse for outro (plus two side bars flying in from ±350px, spring(damping 26, stiffness 170)).
2. **Giant typing with colour trail**: font 90px/1280w, caret 4px orange bar fixed at x=70% of width; text container translateX = caretX − textWidth(typed). Per char age a (frames since typed): color = interpolate(a,[0,3,6,10],[#F5C33B,#F08A3C,#8A5A3C,#333]). 2f per char. Cut in on a bass hit.
3. **Agent log line**: each line enters opacity 0→1, blur 6→0, y +10→0, 8f ease-out; color starts accent #E8792F and interpolates to #111 over 12f once the next line appears; column auto-scroll: translateY to keep last line at 70% height, spring(damping 30, stiffness 120). Bottom "Working…" with shimmer (linear-gradient mask moving 1.2 s/loop).
4. **Picker wheel**: items 22px apart; offset animates through N items over 30f `bezier(0.33,0,0.2,1)` (fast start, soft land); per item opacity = max(0.12, 1 − 0.3·|distance|), selected item black + slight weight bump; icon node fixed left with a 20px connector line.
5. **Generation bloom**: placeholder (grey gradient) → over 20f: overlay iridescent blob (radial-gradients pink #F6B7E6, lilac #C9B6FF, peach #FFD3A8, blur 30px) fades in; then real image fades in under it with blur 24→0 and `saturate(0.3)+hue tint → normal` over 20f; overlay fades out. Total ≈40f.
6. **Choice chips**: 4 chips, stagger 3f, each scale 0.9→1, opacity 0→1, y 8→0, spring(damping 18, stiffness 220); the "picked" chip flashes bg #FCE2CF for 8f then back to white. Camera pushes in on the question beforehand (scale 1→1.6, 24f ease-in-out).
7. **Collapse-to-orb ending**: camera zoom-out 1→0.35 over 30f ease-in; then all cards' positions lerp toward center (ease-in cubic, 15f) with tint to orange (mix-blend overlay) and blur 0→16px; replace with a blurred orange circle that scales 0.2→6 (fills screen) over 20f ease-in-out revealing the bg texture.
8. **Constant camera drift**: never fully still — add 0.5–1%/s scale creep or 10–20 px/s pan to every hold.
9. **Beat-locked timing at 104 BPM (beat = 17.3 f)**: caret→card = 3.5 beats to 96% (≈60 f overdamped spring); hook cut to giant type on the drop, 2–3 f *after* the downbeat; log lines ~0.7 beat apart (12 f); chip stagger 3 f inside a 1-beat window; collapse starts exactly on a beat and lasts 1.5 beats (25 f); logo morph = ~2.8 beats (48 f) starting on the last strong hit.
