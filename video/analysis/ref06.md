# ref06 — "ElevenLabs MCP" launch promo: one chat prompt drives voice, image, music & video generation → a full brand campaign (dark UI, 16:9)

## Format
- 1276×720 (16:9), 30 fps, 44.0 s (1321 frames). H.264 + AAC 44.1 kHz stereo.
- Audio: female/male narrator VO from 0.0 to ~31.5 s over a driving electronic bed at **134 bpm** (beat = 13.4 f); VO stops before the climax; music-only montage 33–36 s; logo sting = soft descending arpeggio/chime 41.8–44 s.
- 13 scene cuts, 5 of them in a 1.8 s montage (33.1–34.9 s); the rest of the film is continuous camera + morphs.

## Animation architecture
- **Layers**: (1) background: near-black #141316 with subtle vignette; sometimes a warm blurred colour field (orange/red) as ambient light behind UI; (2) UI: dark glass panels #1E1D21, 1 px border rgba(255,255,255,0.08), r≈14–18 px; (3) content imagery (generated photos) in rounded cards or full-bleed; (4) cursor layer; (5) typographic layer (giant kinetic prompt).
- **Camera**: 2D virtual camera with frequent zoom-to-component (window → input → button), caret-following pans, pull-back + scroll for overview; one punch-in hard cut; slight 3D tilt on cards.
- **Transition vocabulary**: drum-carousel → squeeze-to-line → grow-window; crop-to-component; colour-sweep "processing"; exposure-flash cut into result; blur-morph (image → input); rack-focus (modal blur ↔ background unblur); click-bloom → montage hard cuts; dissolve to bokeh → CTA; identical logo bookend.
- **Typography choreography**: per-char blur-in for logo and CTA; typewriter with colour-coded keywords (coral #E5806B on white #F2F2F2); trailing-colour caret (new chars coral then white); kinetic giant prompt with caret-follow camera + depth blur. Font: humanist/neo-grotesk (Inter/Söhne-like) regular, never bold; logo in its own wordmark.
- **UI simulation**: typing, drag-and-drop with "+" badge, send button press, modal with list rows + hover highlight, chat thread with image grids & audio players, toggle switch.
- **Depth/lighting**: blur is the main depth cue (background blur 30–40 px behind modals, DOF on text); warm glow behind windows; no grain; images have rich colour against the neutral dark UI.
- **Colour system**: neutral darks (#141316 bg, #1E1D21 panels, #2A292E strokes), white text, coral accent for "AI-highlight" words, blue (#2F7BF6) only for the toggle ON state, pink/violet imagery provides the warmth.

## Pacing & rhythm
- Average shot/idea length ≈ 2 s; result gallery holds 1.2–1.8 s per image; final montage 0.33–0.67 s per shot (accelerating).
- Structure: brand (2.5 s) → capabilities drum (1.5 s) → prompt #1 + attach (5 s) → results (6 s) → prompt #2 (iteration) + modal (6 s) → more results (5 s) → overview scroll (2 s) → kinetic prompt #3 (3 s) → click → montage payoff (2.5 s) → CTA (2.5 s) → logo bookend (5.5 s).
- Tension: three prompts escalate in type size (small UI → zoomed UI → giant kinetic headline); VO drops out at ~31.5 s, music dips (−21…−23 dB, 31.2–32.8 s), the click triggers the loudest section and the fastest cuts.
- Ending: text CTA + integration icons + logo with toggle switched ON.

## Sound & sync
- **Music**: 134 bpm electronic (beat 13.4 f, bar 53.7 f), continuous at −13…−17 dB under VO; breakdown 31.2–32.8 (−21…−23 dB) before the click; energy returns at 32.9 (+5 dB step = riser/whoosh into the click bloom); montage section driven by strong hits 34.14 / 34.59 / 35.07 / 35.52; ending chime arpeggio from 41.8 s.
- **VO** (rough transcript, low confidence): *"Introducing [image,] music and video generation in the ElevenLabs MCP. Describe what you want [and let Claude/your assistant] handle the creative… whether it's a ten-second spot or a thirty-second ad … switch between [voices/models] … [21.8] …create … just for you … [27.4] … take your campaign to … finished assets …"* VO occupies 0.0–31.5 s and stops before the click → the climax is music-only.
- **VO pauses**: loudness dip at 5.6 s (−24.8) = the moment the camera crops to the prompt input; dip 31.2–32.8 = button suspense. New VO sentences start with new UI states (window 4.2, prompt typing 5.8, iteration prompt 15.6, kinetic prompt 28.6).
- **Cut-on-beat**: 6/13 scene cuts within ±2 f of a beat (vs ~30% chance) — the montage cuts 34.20 (+1.8 f), 34.57 (−0.7 f) and gallery cuts 13.70 (−1.8 f), 19.80 (−0.3 f) are on the grid; cuts at 8.47 (−7 f) and 33.13 (−3.5 f) are **intentionally early**: they are "action" cuts (drop/click) that lead the beat. Animation starts: 7/21 on beat; logo/brand moments (0.4, 3.9, 10.1, 22.4, 40.0) are on the grid.
- **SFX**: subtle UI clicks on send-button presses (onsets 18.8–19.0, 32.95), a whoosh/riser into the click bloom (32.9 loudness step), the drag-drop "+" pop near 8.2 (onset + strong hit 8.22).
- **Sync rules**: (1) VO carries the story through UI, (2) cut out VO for the final reveal, (3) dip music 1.5 s (≈ 1 bar + ½) before the hero click, (4) montage cuts on consecutive beats, accelerating, (5) logo sting is a separate musical gesture (chime) after the music ends.

## Reusable recipes
1. **Per-char blur-in logo/CTA**: each char opacity 0→1, blur 6→0 px, translateY 4→0, 6 f easeOutCubic, stagger 1.5 f in shuffled order; accent word in coral starts 4 f later.
2. **Toggle ON**: track #3A3940→#2F7BF6 and knob x 0→14 px over 6 f cubic-bezier(0.65,0,0.35,1); place exactly on a beat (13.4 f grid at 134 bpm).
3. **Capability drum**: N chips on a cylinder (rotateX, radius 60 px, perspective 600); step = 1 beat (13 f), each step 6 f ease-in-out; centre chip scale 1, opacity 1; ±1 neighbours scale 0.85, opacity 0.5, blur 1 px; then squeeze scaleX 1→0.08 in 4 f (ease-in) and grow the next container from that line in 20 f cubic-bezier(0.16,1,0.3,1).
4. **Colour-coded typewriter**: 1 char per 1.7 f; keywords rendered in coral; the last 4–6 typed chars use coral + blur 3 px + scale 0.85, each char settling to white/sharp/1.0 over 7 f easeOutQuad.
5. **Kinetic giant prompt**: font size ~11% of frame height, single line; container translateX = −(caretX − 0.62·W), smoothed with a 6 f lag (critically damped spring: damping 26, stiffness 120, mass 1); apply CSS blur proportional to |x − caret| (0 px within 25% W, up to 4 px at the edges).
6. **Exposure-flash result reveal**: hard cut to image with filter brightness 1.4, saturate 1.6, hue-rotate −20°, scale 1.15; tween to neutral and scale 1 over 8–12 f easeOutCubic; then scale 1→0.88 + radius 0→16 over 20 f to become a card.
7. **Blur-morph back to input**: image blur 0→40 px + brightness 1→0.35 over 12 f while its container animates width/height/radius to the input bar's rect over 10 f ease-in-out; input placeholder/caret appears in last 4 f.
8. **Rack-focus modal exit**: modal opacity 1→0 + blur 0→8 px over 8 f while background blur 30→0 px over 12 f (start together).
9. **Click bloom → montage**: press 0.95 for 2 f, then scale 1→1.6 over 5 f ease-in with radial white glow (0→60% opacity, radius 0→40% W); hard cut on frame 6 to montage; montage shot lengths 20,12,11,10 f (≈1.5,1,1,0.75 beats), each shot with a 1.03→1.0 settle.
10. **Bookend logo**: reuse the exact intro composition as the final 5 s, ending on the "ON" state.
