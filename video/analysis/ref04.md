## Animation architecture
- **Layers**: (1) flat background #E8E8E8 (light) – later swapped for full-bleed blurred gradient or dark #000/#0B1220 space; (2) main subject (logo / cards / orb / chat) always centred; (3) secondary satellites (labels, number, ring text, scattered bubbles). No device frames, no browser chrome – UI fragments float on the canvas.
- **Camera**: 2D virtual camera with scale + translate, plus occasional true 3D (dot grid perspective). Constant subtle drift/follow; big moves are push-in / pull-out *through* objects (fly into orb, exit from orb).
- **Transition vocabulary**: (a) shared-element morph (orb→avatar, toggle→orb, pill→logo), (b) fly-through scale (camera into object so its fill becomes the background), (c) radial iris (dark vignette grows), (d) logo-letter erase/retype, (e) split-apart (number halves part to reveal orb), (f) one hard cut at the half-way point.
- **Typography choreography**: typewriter for everything UI (labels, prompts, pills), per-character rise+blur for numbers, scrambled out-of-order letter pop for logos, tracking-collapse for the partner name. Font: neutral grotesk (Inter-like) medium/regular; UI text tiny (≈1.3% of frame height), headline words in pills ≈4% of frame height.
- **UI simulation**: typing at 2–3 chars/frame for long text, ~1 char/2 frames for short headline words; chat bubbles pop + camera scroll; segmented toggle with sliding highlight; counter ticking; graph highlight-by-dimming.
- **Depth/lighting**: very soft shadows (0 4px 24px rgba(0,0,0,.04)), coloured glow on brand icon (green bloom ~30 px), orb uses fluid gradient + inner white highlight + 1 px white rim. No grain.
- **Colour system**: monochrome light grey canvas + white cards + black text; ONE hero gradient (blue #8FB0E0 / white / orange-red #D2491F) used as orb and later as full background; partner green used only on its icon. Dark section uses navy/teal with pale green-white dots.

## Pacing & rhythm
- Effectively a single continuous shot; "beats" are morphs every 1.5–3 s. Average idea length ≈ 2.5 s.
- Structure: Logo (3.5 s) → Setup (3.5 s) → Inbound call + voice (6 s) → Chat payment (3 s) → Logic graph (2 s) → Orb/dark network climax (6 s) → Channels (2 s) → 32 languages (6 s) → Tagline triad (3 s) → Logo (5 s).
- Tension builds light→dark (21–25 s) then resets with the only hard cut; second half is faster and more numerous (bubbles explode) before simplifying to 3 words → logo.
- Ending: no URL/CTA text, just the logo formed from the last UI element; long 5 s hold with audio fade.

## Reusable recipes
1. **Scrambled logo build**: show mark at scale 1.6 → spring to 1 (damping 20, stiffness 180, ~12 f). Then reveal wordmark letters in a random order, 1 letter every 2 f, each letter opacity 0→1 in 2 f with no movement. Erase = reverse order, 1 letter/f.
2. **Tracking collapse name**: letterSpacing 0.6em → 0 and opacity 0→1 over 10 f, easing bezier(0.16,1,0.3,1); characters stagger 1 f.
3. **Typewriter card stack**: card enters scale 0.96→1, opacity 0→1, y +12→0, 8 f ease-out; label types at 1 char/f, body at 3 chars/f; next card starts when previous text is 80% done; whole container translateY drifts −15 px/s to keep the newest card near centre.
4. **Smash-zoom icon**: icon scale 4→1, blur 20→0 px, opacity 0→1 over 8 f, bezier(0.2,0.9,0.1,1); add drop-shadow glow (brand colour, 30 px, 0.6 opacity) fading to 0.25.
5. **Rolling number**: per char: translateY 10→0 px, blur 4→0, opacity 0→1, 6 f each, 2 f stagger; colour #9A9A9A→#333 after landing.
6. **Orb shared-element**: same React component moves from centre (size 260 px) to avatar slot (size 24 px) with interpolate on x, y, size over 20 f, bezier(0.65,0,0.35,1); destination UI (chat bubbles) fades in during the last 8 f.
7. **Fly-into-object transition**: scale the hero object 1→8 over 12 f (ease-in, bezier(0.7,0,0.84,0)), at scale ≥4 swap to a full-bleed version of its fill as background; new content starts popping in on the last 4 f.
8. **Iris to dark**: radial-gradient mask whose dark radius grows from 0 to 150% of diagonal over 15 f, while subject scales to 0.05; then dot-grid scene fades in.
9. **Word pill staircase**: pill width animates with text length (typing 1 char/2 f); when complete, previous pill scale 1→0.55, translate (−80, −60) px, 10 f spring(damping 22, stiffness 200); new pill enters at centre.
10. **Bars-close logo outro**: two vertical bars translate from ±45% frame width to ±6 px from centre over 15 f (ease-in-out), the content between them scales to 0; bars become the logo mark; then wordmark scramble-builds.
