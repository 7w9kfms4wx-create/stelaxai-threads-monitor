# ref10 — ElevenLabs "Eleven v3/V4" vertical launch film: generated-voice dialogue samples shown as particle-cloud faces with karaoke captions, tied together by one morphing gradient orb

## Format
- 720×1280 (9:16 vertical), 30 fps, 156.3 s (≈4690 frames), stereo AAC 48 kHz.
- Audio: continuous **voice** (generated dialogue samples, voice-band RMS steady −17…−24 dB the whole time) over a soft music bed. Bass swells (lowpass <150 Hz rises from ≈−30 to −11…−14 dB) land exactly on the visual "chapter" moments: 12 s (orb burst into particles), 58–66 s (orb interstitial), 90–102 s (full-screen gradient section, loudest part of the film), 147–152 s (V4 macro type → logo). Last 0.3 s is black/silent.
- Only 3 hard cuts in 156 s (100.2, 148.2, 149.2 s). Everything else is continuous morphing.

## Animation architecture
- **Layers**: (1) flat warm-white bg #F3F1EF, sometimes a radial gradient glow layer; (2) "hero object" layer — a single gradient orb that morphs (pill → orb → dots → bars → particles → face); (3) caption/text layer, small type; (4) foreground bokeh particles (blurred, larger, faster) for depth.
- **Camera**: mostly locked 2D; transitions done as push-ins *through* objects (scale up ×6–10 with ease-in, then the next scene is revealed inside), and pull-outs from macro type. Occasional pseudo-3D (dot field flying toward camera).
- **Transition vocabulary**: morph of the hero object (no cuts), dissolve-into-particles / reassemble-from-particles, push-through-object, pixel mosaic wipe, blur cross-fade for text.
- **Typography**: geometric grotesk (looks like Inter/Eleven's own sans), regular weight, small (2–5% of width) for body; one-word titles ≈5% width. Reveals = per-word blur+opacity wave with whole-line scale settle; karaoke colouring for captions; typewriter for small labels; macro type for the climax.
- **UI simulation**: almost none — product = voice, visualised by (a) waveform bars, (b) particle faces, (c) karaoke captions with emotion tags.
- **Depth/lighting/grain**: soft mesh gradients with slight noise; defocus blur on foreground particles; no hard shadows.
- **Colour**: off-white base, 2-colour gradient brand (orange #F26B3A-ish + deep green #1F4D33-ish + cream), accent red-orange for "current word". Everything else greys.

## Pacing & rhythm
- Very long takes; only 3 cuts. Rhythm comes from caption changes (~3–5 s each) and chapter interstitials every ~40 s (12 s, 52 s, 90 s, 147 s) aligned with bass swells.
- Tension: restrained text open (7 s) → first morph payoff (12 s burst). Mid-point peak at 90–100 s (full-colour, loudest). Final payoff: macro "V4" → logo.
- Ending: 4 s static logo hold, no URL/CTA text.
- Lesson for a 30–45 s outreach video: steal the *hero object that morphs between chapters* and the karaoke caption, not the length.

## Sound & sync
- **Music**: soft electronic/ambient pulse, **125 BPM** (1 beat = **14.4 frames**, 1 bar = 57.6 f). Beat grid runs 0.58 s → 146.0 s, then the music loses its pulse for the outro (146–156 s is a beatless sustained swell + VO). Loudness steady −12…−20 dB under dialogue; swells at 12 s, 58–66 s, **90–102 s (loudest, −9…−11 dB)**, 147–152 s.
- **Voice**: this film is mostly VO/dialogue (generated voices). Reconstructed script (ASR approximate):
  - 0.5–6.6 s: "Everything you are about to hear was generated from the prompts shown, with no edits or modifications — just Eleven v3." → **the VO reads exactly the on-screen text while it reveals** (line 1 reveals 0.27–1.93 s as the words are spoken; line 2 starts 1.95 s on beat).
  - 6.6–148 s: dialogue samples (nervous actor "…okay, I'm ready. [commanding] Rolling", a cheerful Melbourne radio host, "you know, I stood in front of the mirror this morning…", a pharmacy/prior-authorization call that switches to Mandarin, etc.). Each sample's words drive the karaoke caption word highlight.
  - 148.4–150.5 s: "…Eleven v3/V4 … the next frontier of human-level communication" over the macro V4 → lockup.
- **Cut-on-beat ratio**: 1 of 3 hard cuts lands on a beat (100.17 s, −1.6 f from beat & strong hit 100.22). The other 2 cuts (148.2, 149.2) are in the beatless outro and sync to VO instead. Chapter animation starts: orb grow 8.8 s (+1.8 f from strong hit 8.74), face→orb 52.0 s (−0.9 f from strong hit 52.03), line 2 1.95 s (−0.9 f), burst 12.2 s (−4.5 f) → 4 of 6 within ±2 f.
- **SFX**: no obvious whooshes; transitions are carried by **bass swells** (orb burst 12 s, interstitial 58 s, gradient section 90 s). The swell starts ~0.3 s *before* the visual burst (anticipation), visual peak lands on the next beat.
- **Sync rules seen**: (1) on-screen text is revealed at speaking speed while VO reads it; (2) caption highlight follows the spoken word exactly (karaoke); (3) VO pauses between samples (≈0.5–1 s) are where the particle faces dissolve/re-form; (4) brand line spoken over the logo, logo resolves just after the last word.

## Reusable recipes
1. **Blur-wave line reveal** (text in): per word `delay = i*4f`; each word opacity 0→1, blur 8px→0, y +6px→0 over 14f, `Easing.bezier(0.22,1,0.36,1)`; simultaneously the whole line `scale 0.88→1` over 30f same easing. Lines stagger 30–33f. Exit = mirror (blur 0→12px, opacity →0, 12f, 3f per word).
2. **Token → hero orb morph**: element (pill 60×24, r=12) animates `width/height → 90×90`, `borderRadius → 45`, position → center over 24f with spring(damping 20, stiffness 120, mass 1); fill switches to an animated mesh gradient (2–3 radial-gradients whose centers rotate 360° per 6 s); during growth add `filter: blur(2px)` easing to 0.
3. **Dot → waveform bars**: 1 dot splits into N=5 dots (x spread 14px apart) in 3f; each bar `height = 10 + amp_i(t)*120px` with amp from audio (Remotion `useAudioData`/`visualizeAudio`) smoothed; enter with spring(damping 10, stiffness 180) for ~10% overshoot; bar width 10px, radius 5, vertical gradient green→orange.
4. **Particle burst → push-through**: dot explodes into 60 particles, radial velocity 200–600 px/s with ease-out (`1-(1-t)^3`) over 30f; behind them a radial glow (cream center, orange/lime ring) scaling 0.2→3.0 over 30f so its ring leaves the frame; land the bass hit on frame 0 of the burst.
5. **Karaoke caption**: words array with start/end frames; color = past `#B88B8B`(muted), current `#E5482E`, future `#CFCACA`; transition between states 4f linear; bracketed tags 70% opacity. 2 lines max, centered at 82% height, font 14–16px on 720 w (scale to ~3.5% of width for phones).
6. **Push-into-object scene change**: target object centered; `scale 1→8` over 20f `Easing.in(Easing.cubic)`, add blur 0→20px in last 8f; at peak swap scene, next scene starts at blur 20→0, scale 1.15→1 over 12f ease-out.
7. **Macro type → lockup pull-out**: giant glyphs (font-size ≈ 2× frame height), blur 20px→4px while panning 120px diagonally in 18f; then cut to word at scale 3 and pull back to 1 (or 0.3 to become a badge) over 30f `bezier(0.65,0,0.35,1)`.
8. **Lockup assemble**: badge visible; prefix word appears (opacity 0→1 in 2f), group re-centres via translateX spring(damping 22, stiffness 200) ≈10f; then cross-dissolve L→R to final logo, 2f per letter.
9. **Beat-locked timing at 125 BPM (beat = 14.4 f)**: text line reveal = 3.5 beats (50 f) and next line starts on the following beat; hero-object morph = 2.5 beats (34–36 f) starting on a beat; shrink/anticipation = 0.75 beat (11 f) ending on a beat; snap/split = 0.5 beat (7–8 f); chapter interstitial every 8 bars; caption line changes on VO phrase ends, not beats.
