## Animation architecture
- **Layers**: bg alternates between (a) a blurred multi-blob gradient with grain (#D34A1E orange, #9FB2D3 blue, rust/brown) and (b) pure white #FFFFFF. A content layer (tiles, cards, UI), a text layer (single labels, ~14–16px at 720p, black or white), and a mask layer (organic blob iris).
- **Camera**: 2D only; zoom-outs reveal a layout (Logos → bento board), and jump-zooms (hard cuts at 1.6×) follow a caret. Depth is simulated in the orbit section by scale + y + subtle blur on the far tiles.
- **Transition vocabulary**: organic blob iris (white), liquid gradient flow-in, vertical text roll, shape morph (tiles → placeholder → button → tile), blur→focus "AI resolve", converge-to-grid, list push-up.
- **Typography**: tiny, quiet labels (headline ~2.2% of frame height) in a neutral grotesk. A process checklist (label + ✓). Typing with a tinted trailing tail. A shimmer sweep on "in-progress" labels. The tagline is built word by word. All text is centred, with huge whitespace around it.
- **UI simulation**: upload button, drag-drop, prompt box with chip mention + inline thumbnail, AI checklist with shimmer → check, Instagram-post frame.
- **Colour system**: white + black + the brand content colours (deep red #8B1A1A / burgundy, teal #1F8A70, cream). Brand-tinted text accents (red/mauve) are used during typing and then neutralise to black.
- **Grain**: visible film grain on the gradient bg (fine, ~3–5% opacity noise, animated per frame).

## Pacing & rhythm
- Music-locked: first 9 s on a sparse beat with roughly one visual event per hit (~0.75–1.2 s apart). Then the demo section runs on a steady beat with state changes every ~1.5–2 s and a payoff burst at 24 s.
- Three acts: **Intro/brand (0–10 s)** → **Process (10.5–24 s: prompt → reading → matching → applying → creating)** → **Outcomes (24–31.6 s)** → logo (32–37.5 s).
- Tension builds with the checklist (each ✓ is a micro-reward) and the "Creating outputs" hold on an almost empty white frame. The payoff is the asset explosion. It ends on the tagline collapsing into the logo; no CTA button.

## Sound & sync
- **Music**: a modern minimal electronic track at **125 BPM** (beat = 14.4 frames, bar = 57.6 frames). Arrangement: **intro 0–9.3 s** has only a kick/bass thump pattern (hits ≈0.30, 1.50, 2.25, 3.45, 4.15, 5.35, 6.05, 7.25, 8.00, 9.20 s, a 2-hit figure every ~1.93 s ≈ 4 beats). There is a **2-frame silence at 9.85 s** and the **full-band drop at 9.9 s**. A brighter layer enters ~25.5 s (the outputs/use-cases act) and more harmonic density from ~31.5 s. **No fade**: it is still at −11…−17 dB at 37.4 s, and the video ends on the logo hold.
- **VO**: none (the ASR output is music artefacts).
- **Cut-on-beat ratio**: automatic motion onsets fall within ±2 frames of a beat 22% of the time (chance ≈ 28%), and motion peaks 22%. So **most motion is not frame-locked**. What is locked: the intro choreography (bars collide on beat 0.61, open-peak on 1.09, iris from hit 1.79 to beat 2.05, Brand-kit roll on hit 2.75, logo drop 6.87 on hit 6.85) and the hard cuts are near the drop (drop 9.9 s → prompt cut 10.5 s, +0.6 s). After the drop the visuals run on their own ~1.5–2 s state cadence (≈ every 4 beats) over a steady beat.
- **SFX**: no separate SFX layer is detectable; the kick hits act as the "impacts" for intro events.
- **Sync rules to copy**: (1) In a sparse intro, give each kick hit one visual event (logo collide, iris, word roll, burst). (2) Let the drop arrive while the last intro layout is on screen, and cut to the demo ~1 beat-group (4 beats ≈ 0.6–2 s) after it. (3) In the demo, change state every 4 beats (57.6 frames at 125 BPM, rounded to 58), not on every beat. (4) Don't fade the music; end on the logo while it's still playing.

## Reusable recipes
(At 125 BPM: beat = 14.4 frames → use 14/15 alternating; 2 beats = 29 frames; bar = 58 frames.)
1. **Organic blob iris transition (measured)**: a white SVG blob (8 points, jittered by noise per frame) scaled from the centre so the frame is **covered in 8 frames**. The radius follows `Easing.bezier(0.8,0.6,0.1,1)` (13% at f2, 43% at f4, 98% at f8), with `filter: blur(16px)` on the mask edge. Content B sits inside the mask. Start it on a hit so full coverage lands on the next beat.
2. **Gradient-wedge "AI generated" entrance (measured)**: each asset is a container with a `linear-gradient(135deg, c1, c2)` overlay (the asset's two dominant colours). Container `clip-path: polygon` / scale from its top-left corner 0→1 over **7 frames** (`bezier(0.2,0.8,0.2,1)`) with a feathered leading edge (mask-image linear-gradient). Then the image opacity goes 0→1 and the gradient overlay 1→0 over the next **6 frames**. Stagger 3–4 frames (≈¼ beat at 125 BPM), scattered positions; afterwards drift ±6px over 2 s.
3. **Shimmer "AI working" label**: text color #9A9A9A with `background: linear-gradient(90deg, #9A9A9A 0%, #111 45%, #9A9A9A 60%)`, `background-clip:text`, background-position animated −100%→200% over 30 frames, looping. On completion: the text goes solid #111 in 6 frames and a ✓ draws (stroke-dashoffset, 8 frames) 6px to the right.
4. **Progressive checklist**: a max of 2 lines visible; each new line enters at y+16px with opacity 0 (8 frames, ease-out). When a 3rd arrives, the stack translates up by one line height (8 frames) and the top line fades out.
5. **Typing with tinted tail**: 1 char per 1.5–2 frames; each char's colour interpolates accent (#B0607A mauve) → #111 over the next 12 frames, which gives a gradient tail on the last ~6 chars. Mentions become a chip: bg #FBE9DF, text accent, radius 6px, fade in 6 frames, then an inline 40px thumbnail pops (scale 0→1, rotate −6°→0°, spring damping 12, stiffness 200).
6. **Orbiting asset cloud**: N tiles on an ellipse (rx 38% of width, ry 30% of height) with phase offsets. Angle advances ~25°/s. Scale = 0.6 + 0.5·(sin θ+1)/2 and zIndex follows the same depth value; far tiles get blur 1–2px. Entrance: all burst from the centre label (scale 0→1, 6 frames, stagger 1 frame).
7. **Bento board build via zoom-out**: the hero tile starts at scale 1.6 centred, then goes to scale 1 at its grid slot (14 frames, ease-in-out). The 4 other modules fly from offsets of ±150px toward their corners with blur 10→0, opacity 0→1, 10 frames, stagger 3 frames.
8. **Gradient-mesh bg with grain**: 3–4 large radial blobs (orange #D34A1E, blue #9FB2D3, rust #8A3A1E, cream) at 60–90% of frame size, blur 120px, positions drifting on slow sine paths (period 8–12 s). Overlay animated noise (an SVG feTurbulence baseFrequency 0.9, opacity 0.06, seed changes every frame).
9. **Converge-to-grid**: scattered tiles animate to grid positions (3 columns, gap 10px) over 10 frames `bezier(0.65,0,0.35,1)`. Then the columns scroll in alternating directions at 40 px/s. Cells then crossfade to swaps (colour swatches/type specimens) one by one, stagger 3 frames.
