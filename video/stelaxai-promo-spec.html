1. Почему референсы выглядят дорого — 9 правил архитектуры

Не элементы, а то, как ролик устроен. Это повторяется почти во всех 12 видео.

# Правило Где видно Что это значит для нас
1 Один непрерывный кадр вместо склеек. Сцены перетекают друг в друга, одна «камера» летает по одному миру. На 2,5 минуты — 3 резкие склейки ref01, ref04, ref10, ref11 Весь ролик — один мир с одной камерой. Склейка — только на финальную карточку
2 Один главный объект проходит через все главы и превращается. Круг → аватар → точки → полосы звука → логотип ref04, ref07, ref10, ref12 Наш главный объект — точка Stelax: она становится индикатором «печатает…», из него вырастает ответ, ответ становится записью в календаре, запись схлопывается обратно в точку → логотип
3 Результат вырастает из действия. Кнопка «Generate» превращается в готовый плеер, нажатая кнопка стоит на месте, а мир вокруг меняется ref05, ref07 Пузырь «Yes please» отправляется — и из него же вылетает карточка записи и улетает в календарь Fresha
4 Камера работает на смысл. Наезд в момент действия (×1,33 за 16 кадров), отъезд — чтобы показать масштаб, пролёт «внутрь» объекта как переход ref04, ref09, ref10 Наезд на цену и время в ответе; отъезд от телефона к календарю; пролёт сквозь точку в начале
5 Никогда не статично. Даже на «паузе» всё медленно дрейфует, фон живёт (размытые цветные пятна + зерно 3–8 %) ref08, ref09, ref11 Живой фон с зерном; телефон чуть дышит и поворачивается
6 Один фирменный переход повторяется (щель, закрывающиеся полосы) — ролик кажется цельным ref01, ref03, ref09 Наш повторяющийся ход — «точка»: сжаться в точку → раскрыться новой сценой
7 Монтаж подчинён голосу и музыке. Большие переходы — в паузах речи; нужная картинка появляется за 0–6 кадров ДО слова; главные 4–6 моментов попадают пиком движения в удар музыки; движение стартует на треть доли раньше удара все Сетка 120 уд/мин (доля = 15 кадров). Ключевые моменты — на удар
8 Тишина перед развязкой. Музыка обрывается на 0,5–1,3 с, развязка приходит на самой громкой ноте ref05, ref07, ref12 Перед тем как запись падает в календарь — обрыв, затем удар
9 Минимум объектов в кадре, крупно. Один главный объект по центру, шрифт крупный, 1 акцентный цвет все В кадре в каждый момент одна мысль. Текст в чате — крупный, читается на телефоне

3. Сценарий

Формат: 1080×1920 (9:16), 30 кадров/с, 26 с = 780 кадров. Музыка 120 уд/мин → доля = 15 кадров, такт = 60 кадров. Всё ниже — в кадрах от начала и в долях.

Персонализация (меняется для каждого салона): имя владелицы, название салона, имя мастера, услуга, цены, время. Всё приходит в ролик параметрами — один шаблон рендерит сотню персональных роликов.

Для салонов без подключённой системы записи сцена 4 заменяется: карточка улетает не в календарь, а уведомлением на телефон владелицы («New booking request»), голос: «Every booking request — straight to your phone». Иначе ролик обещает то, чего у салона не будет.

7. Как собрать это в Remotion

Модуль Зачем
remotion: useCurrentFrame, interpolate, Easing.bezier, spring, Sequence, Audio, staticFile Основа
@remotion/google-fonts/Inter (или Geist) Шрифт без ручной загрузки
@remotion/motion-blur (CameraMotionBlur) Размытие только на быстрых перелётах (дорогое — не на весь ролик)
@remotion/noise (noise2D) Дрейф пятен фона, «дыхание» объектов
@remotion/shapes, @remotion/paths Капсулы, кольца, рисование галочки
@remotion/lottie Мелкие иконки, если нужны

Animation architecture

  • Layers: (1) bg: either full-bleed photography with a soft blur or a flat warm off-white #F1EFEA; colour washes are heavily blurred (≥100px) versions of the content palette. (2) Objects: one "hero object" at a time (card, deck, tile, orb, row), always centred or balanced on a diagonal pair. (3) Micro-labels: 8–10px grey captions + hairline rule under cards, flag pills (white pill, flag emoji + 9px label). (4) Connectors: 1px #BDBDBD lines with rounded elbows, or long diagonal hairlines that run off-frame.
  • Camera: pure 2D. "Camera" moves are group translates (pans of 30–50% of frame width, ease-in-out ~25 frames) and continuous slow scale-ups of the hero group (1.0→1.7 over 3 s). No 3D tilt. Depth is faked with blur and scale (DOF on spheres) and with soft shadows.
  • Transition vocabulary: (a) morph (text→dot→card, orbs→card, card→sphere, row-cell→banner), (b) light-leak/colour-fog diagonal wipes, (c) stack/merge, (d) split/duplicate with connector, (e) hard cut only at the climax of an acceleration. Morph is the default; a cut is the exception.
  • Typography: one neutral grotesk (Inter/"Eleven" style), regular weight for words, bold only in the logo. Size small and confident (headline ~2.5% of frame height, ~18px at 720p), centred, generous whitespace. Choreography: type-on with per-glyph fade trail; colour tint→neutral settle; logo built by sliding bars; suffix slot-roll.
  • UI simulation: the dashboard is reduced to one row; counters tick; highlight chips fade in; flag pills as metadata. No cursor.
  • Colour system: neutral warm-grey base (#F1EFEA), black text, one accent colour borrowed from the content each moment (orange, lavender, sage, teal). Iridescent multi-hue gradients for "AI/abstract" elements (orbs, app tiles).
  • Grain/lighting: subtle; the cards carry a large soft drop shadow (y≈12px, blur≈30px, ~15% black). The photo bg at the start has a natural grade.

Pacing & rhythm

  • ~16 distinct "beats" in 57 s, so ~3.5 s per idea, but sub-motions happen every 0.5–1 s (feed steps, content swaps, colour fog), so the eye is never idle.
  • Structure: Hook (title on colour, 2 s) → Inputs (cards, 4 s) → Multiply (deck accelerating, 4.5 s → cut) → Distribute (tree, 3 s) → Scale (feed, 6 s) → Audio layer (orbs, 3 s) → Variants (split pairs, 10 s) → Funnel → Proof (dashboard metrics, 3 s) → Montage finale (colour cycling, 8 s) → Bookend title → Logo (8 s incl. family roll).
  • Tension builds through acceleration (deck cadence 10→6 frames) and release through a hard cut to a single calm object. The payoff is the colour-cycling montage + metrics. It ends on the brand with a long, quiet tail and no explicit CTA text.

Sound & sync

  • Music: a mellow, warm electronic/lo-fi pulse at 93.75 BPM (beat = 19.2 frames, bar = 76.8 frames). It is constant under the VO with no drop. There is a pre-cut "suck-out": the level dips to −25…−29 dB at 10.45–10.70 s, then a hit at −14 dB lands exactly on the 10.73 s hard cut. A swell/whoosh rises to −9 dB at 33.35–33.6 s while the cards dissolve into spheres. The music fades out over the last 5 s (52.3→57.5 s).
  • VO (rough ASR, gist reconstructed): 0.2 s "Introducing [Ads Engine]… your best-performing ad runs in one language, even though your brand sells in dozens of markets — that's worldwide revenue left on the table… [ads] in text and in video, localized [into many languages] while preserving the [brand voice]. Animation architecture

  • Layers: Top panel = white #F9FAF5–#FFFFFF product canvas. Bottom panel = presenter video. A caption overlay at the top of the face panel. Cursor layer above the UI.
  • Split-screen system: a single state variable split (0.5 = half/half, 1 = UI full screen). All transitions animate it in ~8–10 frames with ease-in-out and vertical motion blur on the moving edge and content. The UI content is always re-centred within its current panel height.
  • Camera: inside the UI, a virtual camera zooms between "hero type" scale (text 5% of frame height) and "app" scale (1.8%) and pans to wherever the action is (the cursor or the typing line). No 3D.
  • Transition vocabulary: panel push (slide with motion blur), shared-element flight (chips → editor), zoom-dock (hero text → card), horizontal panel push for app navigation, crossfade-in-place for lists.
  • Typography: Inter-like grotesk, black on white. Tags in purple (#9B3FC5-ish) inside 1px purple rounded-rect outlines (radius ~4px, padding 2/6px). Captions are white, small, lowercase-ish, 1–3 words, sitting at the top of the face panel (not the bottom), centred.
  • UI simulation: caret typing, counters, cursor glide + click compress, disabled→enabled buttons, waveform playhead fill, stacking cards.
  • Colour system: white UI + black buttons + one accent (purple tags). The presenter's orange/red lighting is the warm counterpoint. The V4 ring uses a brand multi-colour gradient.

Pacing & rhythm

  • Caption changes every ~0.5 s, and a UI state changes every ~1–2 s. The big structural beat (panel expansion → generate → collapse) repeats every ~4–8 s: 14.4, 18.4, 22.6, 37–44 s.
  • The same mini-loop repeats 4×: say it → type it (hero) → dock into product → go full-screen → generate → back to the face. The repetition teaches the viewer, then the finale breaks the pattern with fast lists (tags, voices, SFX).
  • No explicit CTA. It ends by looping back to the opening chips (built for autoplay feeds).

  1. Breathing split-screen (measured): expand: split 0.5→1.0 over 13 frames with Easing.bezier(0.5,0,0,1) (50% at frame 4). UI content translateY = (split−0.5)·H/2 to stay centred.
  2. Chip flight into editor (FLIP, measured): measure the chip's start rect (hero, 24px font) and end rect (inline, ~12px). Animate x, y and scale (≈0.53) over 22 frames with Easing.bezier(0.3,0,0,1) (or spring({damping:14, stiffness:70, mass:1})), stagger 2–3 frames, top chip first. Surrounding editor text opacity 0→1, blur 6→0, starting 6 frames after the first chip lands.
  3. Hero-type → dock: type the line big (1 char per frame, caret blink 15-frame period, purple tag colour applied on "]"). Hold 10 frames. Then scale 1→0.45 and move to the card slot over 14 frames, ease-in-out, while the card chrome (border, avatar label, waveform row) fades in with 3-frame stagger.
  4. Cursor click: the cursor moves along a quadratic bezier path over 18–22 frames, bezier(0.4,0,0.2,1). Click: cursor scale 1→0.85→1 (6 frames) and button scale 1→0.96→1, background #000→#333→#000.
  5. Waveform playback: ~60 vertical bars (1.5px wide, gap 2px, random heights 20–100%), grey #C8C8C8. A playhead converts bars to #111 left→right over the clip length. The play icon morphs to pause on click.
  6. Disabled→enabled button: background #D9D9D9→#000 and text #FFF opacity 0.6→1 over 6 frames as soon as the input is non-empty.
  7. Crossfade list ticker: a single slot; each item holds 8 frames, with fade + blur(4px) in/out of 3 frames. 7 items ≈ 2.3 s.
  8. Word-by-word line: words appear every 4–5 frames; each word opacity 0→1, blur 6px→0, y +6px→0 over 6 frames, bezier(0.2,0.8,0.2,1).

Animation architecture

  • Layers: bg alternates between (a) a blurred multi-blob gradient with grain (#D34A1E orange, #9FB2D3 blue, rust/brown) and (b) pure white #FFFFFF. A content layer (tiles, cards, UI), a text layer (single labels, ~14–16px at 720p, black or white), and a mask layer (organic blob iris).
  • Camera: 2D only; zoom-outs reveal a layout (Logos → bento board), and jump-zooms (hard cuts at 1.6×) follow a caret. Depth is simulated in the orbit section by scale + y + subtle blur on the far tiles.
  • Transition vocabulary: organic blob iris (white), liquid gradient flow-in, vertical text roll, shape morph (tiles → placeholder → button → tile), blur→focus "AI resolve", converge-to-grid, list push-up.
  • Typography: tiny, quiet labels (headline ~2.2% of frame height) in a neutral grotesk. A process checklist (label + ✓). Typing with a tinted trailing tail. A shimmer sweep on "in-progress" labels. The tagline is built word by word. All text is centred, with huge whitespace around it.
  • UI simulation: upload button, drag-drop, prompt box with chip mention + inline thumbnail, AI checklist with shimmer → check, Instagram-post frame.
  • Colour system: white + black + the brand content colours (deep red #8B1A1A / burgundy, teal #1F8A70, cream). Brand-tinted text accents (red/mauve) are used during typing and then neutralise to black.
  • Grain: visible film grain on the gradient bg (fine, ~3–5% opacity noise, animated per frame).

Pacing & rhythm

  • Music-locked: first 9 s on a sparse beat with roughly one visual event per hit (~0.75–1.2 s apart). Then the demo section runs on a steady beat with state changes every ~1.5–2 s and a payoff burst at 24 s.
  • Three acts: Intro/brand (0–10 s) → Process (10.5–24 s: prompt → reading → matching → applying → creating) → Outcomes (24–31.6 s) → logo (32–37.5 s).
  • Tension builds with the checklist (each ✓ is a micro-reward) and the "Creating outputs" hold on an almost empty white frame. The payoff is the asset explosion. It ends on the tagline collapsing into the logo; no CTA button.

Animation architecture

  • Layers: (1) background: pure white #FDFDFE or full-bleed heavily-blurred photo (bokeh, ~60 px blur) for "magic/generation" moments; (2) soft blue radial glow (#3d7bff, ~400 px radius, 60% → 0) behind the device; (3) device: minimalist line-art phone (black 6 px stroke, no bezel detail early, Dynamic Island added later), always cropped by the frame — never shown whole until the end card; (4) UI content inside device; (5) hero object: the orb (gradient sphere with animated swirl); (6) floating overlays (comment pills, hearts, sparkles); (7) white flash / blur bloom transitions.
  • Camera: 2D only. The "camera" is a crop window over a tall phone: it pans vertically between header/text region and button region (fast 4–5-frame ease-out slides), and does dive-in crops (3× zoom cut) on images. Constant micro-drift (5–20 px/s) keeps every hold alive.
  • Transition vocabulary: hard cuts on beats; 2-frame white flash; morph (button → glass player); blur bloom (small orb grows + blurs to fill frame, next scene de-blurs out of it); blur-out/blur-in for logo/end card.
  • Typography: SF/Inter-like grotesk, regular weight UI text; "live dictation" word trail (new words in light violet, settling to dark grey); typewriter with accelerating cadence for prompts; end card title large regular (not bold), tagline small grey.
  • UI simulation: typing with auto-growing input; gradient "press" sweep on pills; ring pulse for listening; progress bar on player; floating reaction pills + hearts.
  • Depth/lighting: frosted glass (white 20–30%, backdrop blur, 1 px white 40% border, 24 px radius) over blurred photo; coloured glow behind phone; no grain.
  • Colour: white + black line art, one warm hero (orange/peach orb) vs. one cool accent (electric blue glow + lavender/violet UI highlights). Warm/cool complementary pair.

Animation architecture

  • Layers: (1) flat warm-white bg #F3F1EF, sometimes a radial gradient glow layer; (2) "hero object" layer — a single gradient orb that morphs (pill → orb → dots → bars → particles → face); (3) caption/text layer, small type; (4) foreground bokeh particles (blurred, larger, faster) for depth.
  • Camera: mostly locked 2D; transitions done as push-ins through objects (scale up ×6–10 with ease-in, then the next scene is revealed inside), and pull-outs from macro type. Occasional pseudo-3D (dot field flying toward camera).
  • Transition vocabulary: morph of the hero object (no cuts), dissolve-into-particles / reassemble-from-particles, push-through-object, pixel mosaic wipe, blur cross-fade for text.
  • Typography: geometric grotesk (looks like Inter/Eleven's own sans), regular weight, small (2–5% of width) for body; one-word titles ≈5% width. Reveals = per-word blur+opacity wave with whole-line scale settle; karaoke colouring for captions; typewriter for small labels; macro type for the climax.
  • UI simulation: almost none — product = voice, visualised by (a) waveform bars, (b) particle faces, (c) karaoke captions with emotion tags.
  • Depth/lighting/grain: soft mesh gradients with slight noise; defocus blur on foreground particles; no hard shadows.
  • Colour: off-white base, 2-colour gradient brand (orange #F26B3A-ish + deep green #1F4D33-ish + cream), accent red-orange for "current word". Everything else greys.

  • Transition vocabulary: morph (orb↔tile↔button↔bars), zoom-through with blur, slit-collapse (rows squash), radial scatter/gather, coverflow advance, conveyor scroll, gradient-map generation tint, two hard cuts around a music break.

  • Depth/lighting: very soft shadows (0 6 20 rgba(0,0,0,.06)), rounded corners ~12–16 px, radial pastel glow behind important tiles, no grain (cleaner than ref11).
