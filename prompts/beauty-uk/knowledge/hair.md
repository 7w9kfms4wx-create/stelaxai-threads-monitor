# Hair — craft card (UK)
General craft knowledge for the assistant. None of it is a fact about a specific salon: that salon's own menu, prices, durations, products and policies always take priority.

## Services glossary
Times are typical ranges from industry and salon sources. They depend heavily on hair length, thickness and starting colour, and each salon sets its own. Do not quote them as one salon's timings.

1. **Cut & finish / restyle** (aliases: trim, cut and blow-dry, "wet cut" when there's no finish). Shampoo, cut and style. A trim takes the same shape shorter; a restyle changes the shape. Typically 30–75 min. Maintenance: usually every 6–12 weeks (shorter styles more often).
2. **Blow-dry / finish** (blowout, wash & blow). Wash, then a smooth, bouncy or curled finish. Typically 30–60 min, longer for very long or thick hair. Lasts until the next wash.
3. **Hair up / updo / occasion styling** (event hair, prom hair). Pinned or partly pinned styling for an occasion. Typically 45–90 min. One-off.
4. **Bridal hair + trial.** Wedding-day styling. A trial beforehand, usually 2–3 months before (UK stylists suggest about 8 weeks to 4 months). One-off.
5. **Full head colour / tint** (all-over colour, permanent colour). One shade from roots to ends. Typically 1.5–2.5 hours including the finish. Roots usually need redoing every 4–6 weeks.
6. **Root tint / regrowth / root touch-up.** Colour applied only to the new growth. Typically 1–2 hours with a finish. Usually every 4–6 weeks for full grey coverage.
7. **Semi- / demi-permanent colour** (gloss, "wash-in" colour). Deposits colour without much lift and fades gradually. Often chosen for shine, to refresh tone or to soften early greys. Typically 45–90 min. Fades over several weeks.
8. **Toner / gloss / glaze.** A sheer colour over lightened hair to adjust tone (e.g. take out brassiness). Often 20–40 min on its own, or added to a lightening service. Usually lasts about 3–6 weeks, depending on how often you wash.
9. **Highlights / foils** (half head, full head, T-section, "slices"). Sections woven or sliced into foils to lighten them. Gives brighter, more defined lightness and more lift. Typically 1.5–3+ hours. Usually refreshed every 6–10 weeks.
10. **Lowlights.** Like highlights but darker than the base, used to add depth or tone down over-lightened hair. Similar timing to highlights.
11. **Balayage** (hand-painted highlights, "babylights + balayage", "lived-in blonde"). Lightener painted on freehand for a soft, blended result with no harsh regrowth line. Typically 2–4 hours. Lower upkeep: many clients leave 3–6 months between visits, sometimes with a toner in between.
12. **Ombré / sombré.** Darker roots fading to lighter ends; sombré is the softer version. Typically 2–3+ hours. Low upkeep.
13. **Face-framing / money piece.** Brighter pieces around the face only. Typically 1–1.5 hours. Often refreshed every 6–10 weeks.
14. **Bleach / lightening, global / root bleach** (platinum, "bleach and tone"). Lightener on all of the hair, then a toner. Typically 2.5–5 hours. Bleach roots usually every 4–6 weeks. Going from very dark to platinum usually takes several sessions spread over weeks or months.
15. **Colour correction** (fixing box dye, banding, brassiness, uneven colour, going lighter over dark dye). Custom work to repair or change an unwanted result. Sessions often 3–6 hours; often 2–4 appointments a few weeks apart. Usually needs a consultation first.
16. **Colour removal / colour remover / "colour B4"-type service.** Shrinks and removes artificial colour before recolouring. Part of correction. Time varies.
17. **Grey blending.** Softens the line between grey and coloured hair (often with lowlights, highlights or demi-permanent colour) rather than covering every grey. Less obvious regrowth, so usually longer gaps than root tints.
18. **Bond treatment** (Olaplex, K18, "bond builder", "plex"). Strengthens hair from the inside, added to colour or booked on its own. Usually adds 10–30 min. Some brands suggest a stand-alone treatment about monthly; frequency depends on the hair's condition.
19. **Conditioning / gloss treatment / hair mask / scalp treatment.** Moisture or repair treatment, often added to a blow-dry. Typically 10–30 min extra.
20. **Keratin / Brazilian blow-dry / smoothing treatment** (Brazilian blowout, keratin treatment). Smooths frizz and cuts drying time; it does not make hair poker-straight like relaxing. Typically 1.5–4 hours. Usually lasts about 8–16 weeks; providers' claims vary.
21. **Perm / body wave** (modern perm, "beach wave perm"). A chemical treatment that adds lasting curl or wave. Typically 1.5–3 hours. Usually lasts about 3–6 months, then grows out.
22. **Hair extensions: consultation and colour match.** Usually needed before any extensions so the hair can be matched and ordered.
23. **Tape-in extensions.** Thin panels sandwiched onto your own hair. Fitting typically 1–2 hours. Usually moved up every 6–8 weeks; hair can often be reused.
24. **Micro-ring / nano-ring / I-tip extensions.** Small strands attached with rings, with no glue or heat. Fitting typically 2–4 hours. Usually tightened or moved up every 6–8 weeks.
25. **Weft extensions** (sewn-in, beaded weft, hand-tied weft). Rows of hair attached along a braid or bead track. Usually maintained every 6–8 weeks.
26. **Keratin bond / fusion extensions** (pre-bonded, "U-tip"). Strands attached with a keratin bond. The longest fitting, often 3–5 hours. Usually worn for a few months, then removed.
27. **Clip-ins / extension removal.** Clip-ins can be removed by the wearer. Removing permanent methods is a separate service.
28. **Men's cut / barbering** (clipper cut, scissor cut, fade, skin fade, taper, beard trim, hot towel shave). Typically 20–45 min. Fades are usually redone every 2–4 weeks (skin fades every 1–3 weeks); longer scissor cuts every 4–6 weeks.
29. **Kids' cut.** Shorter appointment. Age bands and policies are set by each salon.

## Questions that pick the right service
- **Cut / restyle:** 1) How long is your hair now and how much do you want off, or is it a big change? 2) Do you have a photo of the look you want? 3) Would you like it blow-dried/styled, or cut only?
- **Blow-dry / updo / event:** 1) What's the date and time of the event? 2) How long and thick is your hair, and do you have extensions? 3) Do you have a photo or an outfit/dress in mind?
- **"I want to go lighter / blonder":** 1) What's your hair colour right now, and is it natural or coloured? 2) Any colour in the last 2–3 years, including box dye, henna or "natural" dyes? 3) Do you want soft and blended (balayage), brighter and defined (highlights), or all-over blonde? Photo please.
- **Grey coverage:** 1) Do you want greys fully covered or softened/blended? 2) Roughly what percentage is grey, and is it currently coloured? 3) How often are you happy to come in?
- **Balayage vs highlights:** 1) How often do you want to visit: every 6–8 weeks or every few months? 2) Natural, blended or bright and bold? 3) Current colour and colour history.
- **Colour correction / "I've had a disaster":** 1) What was used, when, and was it box dye, henna or salon colour? 2) A photo now (in daylight) and a photo of the goal. 3) Any event date? Correction may need more than one visit.
- **Toner / gloss:** 1) Is your hair already lightened? 2) What do you want to change: brassiness, shine, a softer tone?
- **Going darker / back to natural:** 1) Current colour and whether it's bleached. 2) Shade you're aiming for (photo). 3) Do you plan to grow it out?
- **Keratin / smoothing:** 1) Have you had relaxers, perms, keratin or bleach before? 2) Do you want less frizz or straighter hair? 3) Any event coming up? Some treatments need you to avoid washing for a few days.
- **Perm:** 1) Is your hair coloured, bleached or previously permed/relaxed? 2) What kind of curl or wave (photo)? 3) Length now.
- **Extensions:** 1) What do you want: length, volume or both? 2) Your hair's length, thickness and current colour. 3) Have you had extensions before, which method, and how often can you come back for maintenance?
- **Bond treatment / condition:** 1) Is your hair bleached, coloured or heat-styled a lot? 2) What's bothering you: breakage, dryness, frizz?
- **Bridal:** 1) Wedding date, location and start time. 2) How many people need hair done? 3) When could you do a trial?
- **Men's / barbering:** 1) Clippers or scissors, and which grade or fade? 2) Beard work too? 3) Photo, or "same as last time"?

## Common client questions — good general answers
1. **What's the difference between balayage and highlights?** Highlights are done in foils for brighter, more defined lightness; balayage is painted on freehand for a softer, blended look that grows out more gently. Your stylist can recommend which suits your hair and how often you'd like to come in.
2. **Do I need a patch test?** For most permanent and semi-permanent colour, yes. It's usually a skin allergy test done at least 48 hours before your appointment. Each salon has its own policy, so I'll check what they need from you.
3. **Why 48 hours?** A reaction to hair dye can take up to 48 hours (sometimes longer) to show, so the test needs that time to be useful.
4. **I had a patch test last year, do I need another?** Possibly. Salons and colour brands usually ask for a re-test regularly, often every 6–12 months, or sooner if you've used a different colour (including box dye) since. The salon will confirm.
5. **I've used the same colour for years, surely I'm fine?** Allergies can develop at any point, even to a product you've used many times, which is why salons keep re-testing.
6. **What's a strand test?** A small test on a few strands of hair to see how it lifts or takes colour. It's especially useful after box dye or henna, or before big lightening.
7. **I've used box dye. Is that a problem?** Not necessarily, but please tell the stylist. Home dye can affect how hair lightens and may change the plan or the number of sessions needed.
8. **I've used henna.** Thanks for mentioning it! Henna, especially "compound" henna that may contain metallic salts, can react with bleach and some colours, so the stylist will want to check first, usually with a strand test.
9. **Can I go from black to platinum in one go?** Usually not safely. Big lifts are normally done over several sessions to look after your hair. The stylist will plan it with you at a consultation.
10. **Why does colour correction cost more?** It usually takes much longer, uses more product and needs more specialist skill, and often several sessions. That's why salons usually start with a consultation.
11. **How long will my colour last?** It depends on the type of colour, your hair and your aftercare. Toners and glosses fade over a few weeks; permanent colour lasts but your roots grow through.
12. **How often do I need my roots done?** For full grey coverage, many people come back every 4–6 weeks. Grey blending or balayage can stretch that out.
13. **What's grey blending?** Instead of covering every grey, the colour softens them so regrowth is less obvious. It's often lower upkeep.
14. **What does Olaplex / K18 do?** They're bond treatments that strengthen the hair's internal structure. They're often added to colour or bleach services, or booked on their own for damaged hair.
15. **Will a keratin treatment make my hair straight?** It mainly smooths frizz and speeds up drying. Most people find it softens rather than poker-straightens. It usually lasts a few months.
16. **Can I wash my hair after keratin?** Many treatments ask you to wait around 72 hours, but it depends on the product, so follow your stylist's instructions.
17. **How long does a perm last?** Usually a few months, often 3–6. It grows out rather than dropping out completely, and aftercare makes a big difference.
18. **Which extensions are best for me?** It depends on your hair's thickness, the look you want and how much maintenance you're happy with. A consultation and colour match is the best first step.
19. **How often do extensions need maintenance?** Most bonded, tape and ring methods need a refit or move-up about every 6–8 weeks.
20. **Should I come with clean or dirty hair?** It depends on the service: day-old hair is often preferred for updos, while some colour and cuts are fine either way. The salon's own instructions win, so I can check.
21. **When should I book my bridal trial?** Many stylists suggest about 2–3 months before the wedding. Bring photos and details of your dress and accessories.
22. **When should I book hair for an event?** As early as you can for popular dates. For colour, try to have it done about a week before so it settles, and allow time for a patch test.
23. **Can I bring a photo?** Yes please! Photos of what you love (and what you don't) are really helpful. Your stylist will explain how close they can get with your hair.
24. **Will it look exactly like the photo?** The stylist will aim for the look, but the result depends on your hair's colour, history and texture, so they'll talk you through what's realistic.
25. **Is it okay to colour my hair while pregnant?** That's a question for your stylist and, if you're unsure, your midwife or GP. Do mention it when you book so they can advise.
26. **I had a reaction to hair dye before.** Thank you for telling me, that's really important. I'll flag it so the stylist can advise before anything is booked.
27. **How long will my appointment take?** It depends on the service and your hair's length and thickness. The salon's booking system shows its own timings, or I can check for you.
28. **My hair is damaged, can you still lighten it?** It may be possible, but the stylist needs to see it first. They might suggest a strand test or a treatment plan before lightening.
29. **Why do I need a consultation?** For bigger changes it lets the stylist check your hair, colour history and goals, do any tests and give you an accurate plan.
30. **How often should I get a trim?** Many people come every 6–12 weeks; shorter styles and fades need more frequent visits.
31. **Can I go darker easily?** Going darker is usually simpler than going lighter. If your hair is bleached, it may need extra steps so the colour takes evenly.

## Before the appointment (general)
General guidance only. The salon's own instructions always win.
- **Skin allergy (patch) test:** for permanent, semi- and demi-permanent colour, book it at least 48 hours before the appointment. New clients and anyone who has used a different colour (including box dye) since their last test will usually need one.
- **Tell the salon** about any previous reaction, black henna tattoo, scalp problems, pregnancy, medication for allergies, or recent home colour or henna.
- **Colour history:** be ready to share the last 2–3 years, including box dye, henna, keratin, perms or relaxers.
- **Photos:** bring photos of the goal (and of looks you don't want). Daylight photos of your current hair help remote enquiries.
- **Hair washing:** varies by service. Day-old hair is often preferred for updos; some stylists like clean, product-free hair for extensions or keratin. Check with the salon.
- **Events:** allow time for any tests, a possible extra session, and colour to settle (often about a week before).
- **Bridal:** bring dress photos and accessories to the trial.
- **Extensions:** a consultation or colour match is usually needed, and hair may need to be ordered in.

## Aftercare (general)
- **Colour:** many brands suggest waiting about 48 hours before the first shampoo. Use colour-safe, sulphate-free products, lukewarm or cool water, and heat protection. Sun, chlorine and frequent washing speed up fading.
- **Blondes / toner:** purple or blue shampoo can help with brassiness. Toners fade, so a gloss between visits is common.
- **Lightened hair:** bond treatments, masks and gentle heat help. Be careful with brushing when wet.
- **Keratin / smoothing:** often no washing, tying up or tucking behind ears for about 72 hours (depends on the product), then sulphate-free shampoo.
- **Perm:** usually no washing or heat styling for at least 48 hours, then curl-friendly, sulphate-free products.
- **Extensions:** brush from the ends up with a suitable brush, avoid putting conditioner on the bonds/tapes, sleep with hair tied loosely or plaited, and keep up maintenance appointments.
- **Cuts / fades:** regular trims keep the shape.
- **After colour, if skin becomes itchy, red, swollen or blistered:** stop using products and seek medical advice (pharmacist, GP or NHS 111). Breathing difficulty or swelling of the throat or tongue is an emergency: call 999. Let the salon know as well.

## Health & safety signals → stylist decides
The assistant does not judge suitability. It flags the issue, stays kind and neutral, and routes the client to the stylist or salon (and to a medical professional where shown).
- **Previous reaction to hair dye** (itching, swelling, rash, burning). Colour may be unsafe; the stylist decides, possibly with medical advice.
- **Black henna tattoo (now or in the past).** These often contain high levels of PPD and can sensitise people to hair dye.
- **Reaction during or after a patch test.** No colour should go ahead; the stylist must know.
- **Current reaction after a colour** (swelling, blistering, weeping, eyes swelling). Send to medical help: 999 for breathing or throat symptoms, otherwise NHS 111, a pharmacist or GP. Then inform the salon.
- **Scalp conditions** (psoriasis, eczema, broken, sore or sunburnt scalp, open cuts). Chemicals may irritate; the stylist needs to see it.
- **Pregnancy or breastfeeding.** The NHS says most research suggests colour is safe, but the stylist will advise (often patch and strand tests); refer medical worries to a midwife or GP.
- **Hair loss, thinning, bald patches or breakage.** Could have a medical cause; never suggest a cause or treatment. The stylist advises on services and a GP or trichologist on the cause.
- **Allergies, asthma or antihistamine use.** Antihistamines may mask a patch-test result; respiratory issues matter for some treatments (e.g. keratin fumes).
- **Medical treatment** (chemotherapy, recent scalp surgery, strong medication). The stylist decides, possibly with medical clearance.
- **Henna, compound henna, "natural" or metallic dyes, colour-restoring products.** Can react with bleach or perm lotion; usually needs a strand test.
- **Heavily damaged, over-processed or already-broken hair.** Further chemical work may not be safe; the stylist assesses.
- **Recent relaxer, perm or keratin with a colour or bleach request.** Combining chemical services needs a professional plan.
- **Tension, soreness or headaches from extensions or braids.** The stylist should check; possible traction damage.
- **Under-16 asking for colour.** See below; the salon's policy applies.

## Age & legal points (UK)
- **Hair dye under 16:** UK law (retained EU cosmetics regulation, Reg. (EC) 1223/2009 and its annexes) requires oxidative and certain other hair dyes to carry the label warning "This product is not intended for use on persons under the age of 16." HABIA says colouring under-16s goes against the manufacturer's legal labelling and would likely be considered negligent. The NHBF says allergy alert tests and colour must not be carried out on under-16s with PPD-type colour. This is a label requirement and industry standard rather than an outright statutory ban on salons, but in practice most UK salons will not colour under-16s with these products. Policies (e.g. semi-permanent, PPD-free or pastel options) vary; route to the salon.
- **Formaldehyde:** formaldehyde is banned in cosmetic products in the UK. OPSS has recalled keratin products containing it.
- **Patch testing:** no single statute sets the 48-hour test, but manufacturers' instructions and NHBF protocols require it, insurers commonly make it a condition of cover, and salons have a duty of care. Re-test frequency varies: NHBF protocols say every 6 months for existing clients; Wella's record card says at least annually; a summary of L'Oréal Professionnel guidance says after 12 months, or after 6 months without colour.
- **Consent for minors:** no specific UK law found setting a minimum age for haircuts, extensions or perms. Salon policies (e.g. a parent or guardian present) apply.

## Natural add-ons
- Colour or lightening → bond treatment, toner/gloss, cut & finish, deep-conditioning mask.
- Balayage/highlights → toner, face-framing pieces, between-visit gloss.
- Cut → blow-dry or styling, conditioning treatment, fringe trims between visits.
- Blow-dry → treatment or mask, curls/waves, hair up.
- Keratin → trim beforehand (often done the same day).
- Extensions → consultation/colour match, blend cut, maintenance booking, aftercare products.
- Bridal → trial, bridesmaids/mother-of-the-bride styling, accessories fitting, colour a week or so before.
- Men's → beard trim/shape-up, hot towel, wash.
- Any colour → skin allergy test booked at least 48 hours ahead.

## Never promise
- An exact shade, or "it will look the same as the photo".
- Going from dark or box-dyed hair to light blonde or platinum in one session.
- How long colour, toner, keratin, a perm or extensions will last for this client.
- That a colour correction will be fixed in one visit, or a fixed number of sessions.
- That a patch test isn't needed, or that someone is "fine" because they've used the dye before.
- That a client is suitable for a chemical service (that's the stylist's decision after consultation and tests).
- That colour is safe in pregnancy, or anything medical about reactions, hair loss or scalp conditions.
- That bond treatments or keratin will "repair" or "fix" damage completely.
- That treatments are formaldehyde-free, PPD-free, vegan, organic, or use a particular brand, unless the salon has confirmed it.
- Any price, duration, stylist or availability that the salon hasn't provided.
- That under-16s can have colour.
