## Nails — craft card (UK)
- https://www.skinhealthinfo.org.uk/dermatologists-issue-warning-about-uk-artificial-nail-allergy-epidemic/ — British Association of Dermatologists warning on (meth)acrylate nail allergy, symptoms and risk factors (home kits, under-curing).

- https://www.cosmeticsdesign-europe.com/Article/2024/01/03/Nail-acrylates-like-HEMA-are-potent-allergens-that-cause-ACD — HEMA as a potent allergen.

- https://www.womanandhome.com/beauty/biab-vs-acrylic-nails and https://womanandhome.com/beauty/biab-vs-gel-nails — acrylic vs BIAB vs gel definitions.
- https://secretspa.co.uk/guide-to-gel-manicure and https://salongeek.com/threads/how-long-do-you-tell-customers-that-a-gel-manicure-lasts.304408 — gel wear about 2–3 weeks, technicians quote ~2 weeks, regrowth prompts the redo, don't peel.
- https://www.treatwell.co.uk/treatment-files?p=31715 and https://booksy.com/en-gb/50806_the-nail-kitchen_nail-salon_628476_manchester — acrylic infills every 2–3 weeks, an example salon infill cut-off of 4 weeks, and a surcharge for other salons' work.
